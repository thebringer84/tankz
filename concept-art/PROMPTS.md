## Shared direction

Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: full-screen widescreen 16:9 browser game concept art, one screen only. Working title TANKZ. Art direction: gritty military industrial indie game, stylized actual 3D meshes with beautiful hand-painted albedo texture, chunky compact tracked tanks, worn olive armor, sand ochre, dark charcoal iron UI, warm ivory condensed utilitarian typography, restrained amber selections, cyan friendly markers and rusty red enemy markers with shapes to distinguish teams. Painterly surfaces but crisp mesh silhouettes, physically believable light and contact shadows, readable deliberate composition. Rich polished independent game aesthetic, no photoreal soldiers, no glossy sci-fi, no mobile cartoon, no tiny illegible decorative text. UI should be feasible HTML/CSS over Three.js.
